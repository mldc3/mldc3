# María Lourdes Domínguez Cacho

Final-year Physics student at the University of Alicante focused on **quantum technologies**, **topological matter** and **scientific computing**.

My strongest interests are superconducting quantum devices, Majorana physics, Bogoliubov–de Gennes simulations, quantum software and experimental data analysis.

## Featured work

### Majorana–BdG Nanowire Simulations

Research-style Python simulations of one-dimensional topological superconductors, from the Kitaev chain to Rashba–Zeeman Bogoliubov–de Gennes nanowires with induced and self-consistent superconducting gaps.

**Focus:** BdG Hamiltonians, LDOS, finite spectra, topological phase diagrams, particle–hole diagnostics, Majorana decomposition and careful interpretation of near-zero modes.

Repository: [majorana-bdg-nanowire-simulations](https://github.com/mldc3/majorana-bdg-nanowire-simulations)

---

### SSH Acoustic Band Reconstruction

Python pipeline for analysing experimental spectra from coupled acoustic resonators used as SSH-inspired analogue systems.

**Focus:** resonance-peak detection, Lorentzian fitting, band reconstruction, missing-mode completion, interpolation and comparison with topological SSH features.

Repository: [ssh-acoustic-band-reconstruction](https://github.com/mldc3/ssh-acoustic-band-reconstruction)

---

### Scientific Computing Projects

Additional numerical projects in Schrödinger wave-packet propagation, heat-equation solvers, molecular dynamics, hyperbolic PDEs, gravitational lensing and N-body dynamics.
